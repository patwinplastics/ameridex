UNDERSTAND THE WATER PATH

Above-Joist Deck Drainage: Water Stops Above the Framing

Above-joist drainage intercepts water above the deck framing. It can be a separate layer beneath the boards or, with AmeriDex, a board-and-seal assembly integrated into the decking surface.

Request free samples

How AmeriDex manages water at the surface

Cellular PVC boards work with the Dexerdry TPE seal, installed between the boards as the deck is built. The assembly is designed to direct surface water toward the planned discharge edge.

The seal is only part of the installation. Follow the current requirements for pitch, board direction, fastening, flashing, overhang, and sealing at the specified perimeter or end details.

See the two-part system and installation overview

Above-joist does not always mean surface-integrated

Separate membranes or troughs may sit above the joists, below the deck boards. Below-joist systems instead collect water underneath the framing. AmeriDex differs by combining the decking and drainage assembly at the walking surface.

Compare the three drainage locations

For new decks and qualifying full re-decks

AmeriDex cannot be added to boards that are already fastened in place. A full re-deck may be suitable after the old boards are removed and a builder confirms that the existing framing and drainage details meet the installation requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What does above-joist drainage mean?

The water-management layer is above the framing. It may be a separate system below the boards or a surface-integrated assembly such as AmeriDex boards with the Dexerdry seal.

Can I use the system on existing framing?

Possibly, as part of a full re-deck. Remove the existing boards and have a qualified builder assess framing condition, pitch, spacing, flashing, and installation details before specifying the system.

Does drainage make an outdoor space weatherproof?

Deck drainage manages water from above. Wind-driven rain can enter open sides, and drainage does not replace structural inspection, maintenance, or appropriate building-envelope details.
