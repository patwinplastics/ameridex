Dog Area Under a Deck: A Dry, Shaded Spot for Your Dog

The patio under a second-story deck can be a shaded, covered spot for your dog, as long as water is not dripping through the boards. With an AmeriDex deck, rain sheds off the top, so the dog area below starts with a dry space overhead.

Why most under deck dog spots get wet and muddy

Standard deck boards are installed with gaps between them. Every time it rains, water drips through those gaps onto the dog bed, the bowls, and the ground below, and the spot your dog likes best turns wet or muddy. AmeriDex boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so the deck surface sheds the rain instead.

Dog area planning checklist

  • ✓Slope the deck at least 1/8 inch per foot away from the house (see how much slope a deck needs).
  • ✓If boards run away from the house, the framing leaves room for a gutter at the house. Plan the dog area around it (see where the water goes).
  • ✓Keep the dog bed, food, and water bowls back from the outer edge, where rain sheds past the drip edge.
  • ✓Choose a ground surface you can sweep and rinse, such as a concrete or paver patio.
  • ✓If you add gates or fence panels, check your local rules and any HOA requirements first.
  • ✓Shade is not a substitute for fresh water and checking on your dog in hot weather.
  • ✓If you add a ceiling, use a vented vinyl or PVC soffit with at least 1 1/2 inches of continuous ventilated screen at the fascia. Wood ceilings are not recommended.
  • ✓Lights or a fan go in with outdoor-rated fixtures, installed by a licensed electrician (see under deck lighting ideas).
  • ✓Keep it an outdoor space. Never install the system over interior living space.

Dog area under a deck FAQs

Can you make a dog area under a deck?

Yes, when the deck above keeps the rain off. AmeriDex is made for decks, porches, and balconies with a patio, porch, or living space underneath. The boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so rain sheds off the top of the deck instead of dripping through the gaps onto your dog, its bed, and its bowls.

Will the dog area stay dry when it rains?

The deck overhead sheds rain off the top, so the space under it is a dry space. Wind-driven rain can still blow in from the open sides, and rain sheds past the outer drip edge, so keep the dog bed, food, and water bowls back from that edge.

What ground surface works best under a deck for a dog?

Pick a surface you can sweep and rinse, such as a concrete or paver patio. Because the deck sheds the rain, the patio below is not soaked by drips through the boards, which helps keep the area from turning muddy.

Do I need an under deck drainage tray over a dog area?

Not with AmeriDex. The deck surface itself sheds the rain, so nothing has to hang under the joists to catch water, hair, or debris. You can leave the joists open or add a vented vinyl or PVC soffit as a finished ceiling.

Is shade under a deck enough for my dog on hot days?

Shade helps, but it is not the whole plan. Always keep fresh water out, check on your dog in hot weather, and follow your veterinarian's advice for heat. If you add a fan, use an outdoor-rated fixture installed by a licensed electrician.

Can I add a dog area under my existing deck?

Often, as a re-deck, if the existing framing meets the pitch and overhang requirements. The old boards come off and AmeriDex boards with the Dexerdry seal go on. A new deck lets you plan the slope, gutters, and dog area together from the start.
