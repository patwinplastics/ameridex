Outdoor Kitchen Under a Deck: How to Keep It Dry

The patio under a second-story deck is a natural spot for an outdoor kitchen and dining area, as long as rain is not dripping onto the counters. With an AmeriDex deck, rain sheds off the top instead of falling through the boards, so the kitchen below starts with a dry space overhead.

Why most under deck kitchens get wet

Standard deck boards are installed with gaps between them. Every time it rains, water drips through those gaps onto the counters, cabinets, and table below. AmeriDex boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so the deck surface sheds the rain instead.

  • ✓Slope the deck at least 1/8 inch per foot away from the house (see how much slope a deck needs).

Frequently asked questions

Can you put an outdoor kitchen under a deck?

Yes, when the deck above keeps the rain off. AmeriDex is made for decks, porches, and balconies with a patio, porch, or living space underneath. The boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so rain sheds off the top of the deck instead of dripping through the gaps onto counters, cabinets, and the table below.

Can I use a grill under my deck?

Only if the grill maker allows it and your local fire code does too. Follow the manufacturer's clearance instructions for overhead and side clearance, and check with your local building or fire official before you place any grill, smoker, or cooktop under a deck. If you are not sure, keep cooking out from under the deck and use the covered area for prep and dining.

Do I need an under deck drainage tray for an outdoor kitchen below?

Not with AmeriDex. The deck surface itself sheds the rain, so nothing has to hang under the joists to catch water. You can leave the joists open or add a vented vinyl or PVC soffit as a finished ceiling.

What kind of ceiling should go over an under deck kitchen?

If you add a ceiling, use a vented vinyl or PVC soffit with at least 1 1/2 inches of continuous ventilated screen at the fascia. Wood ceilings are not recommended. A finished ceiling is also a clean place to run lights or a fan, installed by a licensed electrician with fixtures rated for outdoor use.

Can I add an outdoor kitchen under my existing deck?

Often, as a re-deck, if the existing framing meets the pitch and overhang requirements. The old boards come off and AmeriDex boards with the Dexerdry seal go on. A new deck lets you plan the slope, gutters, and kitchen layout together from the start.

Does the deck need to be sloped?

Yes. Slope the deck at least 1/8 inch per foot away from the house so rain runs to the outer edge. If the boards run away from the house, the framing leaves room for a gutter at the house.
