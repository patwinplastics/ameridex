Under Deck Ideas for an Outdoor Gym

Home Gym Under a Deck: Work Out in a Dry Space Below Your Deck

The space under a raised deck can be a covered outdoor gym with fresh air and shade, as long as rain is not dripping through the boards onto your mats and weights. With an AmeriDex deck, rain sheds off the top, so your workout zone starts with a dry space overhead.

Why a Gym Under a Standard Deck Gets Wet

Standard deck boards are installed with gaps between them. Every time it rains, water drips through those gaps onto benches, mats, and racks. AmeriDex boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so the deck surface sheds the rain instead.

Home Gym Under a Deck FAQs

Can you put a home gym under a deck?

Many homeowners use the dry space under a raised or second-story deck for a covered outdoor gym. With AmeriDex, the boards lock onto the Dexerdry seal, so rain sheds off the top of the deck instead of dripping through the gaps onto you and your equipment. Plan the floor, headroom, and power with your builder, and check your local code before you build.

Will my workout equipment stay dry under the deck?

The deck overhead sheds rain off the top, so the space under it is a dry space. Wind-driven rain can still blow in from the open sides, and rain sheds past the outer drip edge, so keep equipment back from that edge and choose gear rated for outdoor use.

How much headroom do I need for a gym under a deck?

It depends on how you train, your site, and your local code. Measure for your tallest moves, such as jumping, overhead presses, or a pull-up bar, and plan the deck height and beam locations with your builder from the start.

Do I need an under deck drainage tray over a home gym?

Not with AmeriDex. The deck surface itself sheds the rain, so nothing has to hang under the joists to catch water. You can leave the joists open or add a vented vinyl or PVC soffit as a finished ceiling.

Can I add a ceiling, fans, and lights over the gym?

Yes. If you add a ceiling, use a vented vinyl or PVC soffit with at least 1 1/2 inches of continuous ventilated screen at the fascia. Wood ceilings are not recommended. Use outdoor-rated fans and lights installed by a licensed electrician, keep the sides open for airflow, and do not enclose the space as an interior room.

Can I turn the space under my existing deck into a home gym?

Often, as a re-deck, if the existing framing meets the pitch and overhang requirements and is suitable for the space below. The old boards come off and AmeriDex boards with the Dexerdry seal go on. A new deck lets you plan the slope, gutters, posts, and headroom together from the start.
