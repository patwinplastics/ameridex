The premium upgrade

TrueGrain Premium Collection

The top of the line AmeriDex upgrade. TrueGrain starts with the same cellular PVC core as every AmeriDex board, then adds a proprietary laminate for an ultra-realistic wood look, built to outlast wood.

What makes it TrueGrain

  • Real grain, not a stampA variegated wood-grain laminate carries grain and tone across every board, not a stamped pattern that repeats.
  • Cool underfootA reflective formulation helps keep the surface comfortable for bare feet on sunny days.
  • The same proven coreThe same cellular PVC core as every AmeriDex board, with no wood fiber to rot, on the system that keeps the space under your deck dry.

Five layers on top of a proven core

The proprietary TrueGrain laminate is five bonded layers, each with one job.

  1. 1
    Scratch-resistant top layerA clear, hard wear surface that stands up to foot traffic, patio furniture, and pets.
  2. 2
    UV protection layerShields the wood-grain print and the layers beneath it from the sun.
  3. 3
    Rotogravure wood-grain printA high-resolution print with the grain, pores, and tonal shifts of real hardwood.
  4. 4
    PMMA base with solar-reflective technologyAn acrylic base that reflects part of the sun's heat and gives the laminate a stable backbone.
  5. 5
    Bonding layerLocks the laminate permanently to the cellular PVC board.

TrueGrain questions

Is TrueGrain a different product from AmeriDex?

No. The TrueGrain Premium Collection is the top of the line AmeriDex tier. It starts with the same cellular PVC core as every AmeriDex board and adds a proprietary laminate for an ultra-realistic wood look.

How does TrueGrain get such a realistic wood look?

A variegated wood-grain laminate with a high-resolution rotogravure print carries grain, pores, and tone across the board, so it reads like real wood instead of a stamped pattern that repeats.

Do TrueGrain boards install differently?

No. They install like every AmeriDex board: face screwed with Starborn screws, two per joist, and finished with color-matched plugs.

How much does TrueGrain cost?

Pricing depends on your deck. Ask your dealer or request a quote for TrueGrain pricing.

Are TrueGrain samples free?

Yes. All AmeriDex samples are free, including all six TrueGrain Premium Collection colors.
