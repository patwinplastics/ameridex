Above-Joist vs Below-Joist Deck Drainage

Compare where a system intercepts water, how it is installed, and whether it suits your project. Surface-integrated drainage and separate above-joist systems are different approaches; neither should be confused with below-joist collection.

QuestionSurface-integratedSeparate above-joistBelow-joist
Where is water intercepted?At the decking surfaceAbove the framing, below the deckingBelow the joists
What forms the drainage assembly?Deck boards and a compatible integrated sealA separate membrane or trough assemblyPanels, channels, or other collection components
When is it installed?With the deck boardsBefore the deck boards cover the systemBelow the framing, where the selected system and access permit
Can installed boards remain?No, not for AmeriDex installationGenerally requires deck-surface access; check the system instructionsSome systems allow this; check product requirements
What does a fair quote include?The same project scope: decking, water management, fasteners, edge details, labor, framing, freight, and tax.

Where AmeriDex fits

AmeriDex pairs cellular PVC decking with the Dexerdry seal at the surface. It is intended for new decks and qualifying full re-decks, not insertion into boards that remain installed.

Have the builder confirm framing, pitch, flashing, board direction, fastening, and discharge details. Proper installation is essential, and open-sided areas can still receive wind-driven rain.

Choose the right approach for the work you are planning

If the deck surface will stay in place, review compatible below-joist options. If you are building or replacing the surface, compare both surface-integrated and separate above-joist approaches before committing to the build details.

Frequently asked questions

Do separate drainage systems always leave framing exposed to water?

No. Separate above-joist systems can intercept water above the covered framing. Below-joist systems collect it after it passes through the deck and framing area.

Is the cheapest drainage material the lowest-cost project?

Not necessarily. Compare the same scope, installation work, accessories, finishing details, and maintenance requirements. Request a written project-specific quote.

Can AmeriDex be part of a re-deck?

Yes, when all existing boards are removed and the framing and installation details qualify. It is not an add-on to an already-fastened deck surface.
