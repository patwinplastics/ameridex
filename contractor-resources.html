Before the first board

A better pre-install conversation.

  • Confirm whether this is a new deck or a full re-deck with the old boards removed.
  • Have the builder review framing condition, spacing, pitch, flashing, and fastening.
  • Resolve perimeter discharge, wall connections, transitions, and penetrations.
  • Confirm the exact board, seal, accessories, and applicable document revisions.
  • Review the intended use below, including exposure to wind-driven rain.
Planning checklist only. It is not a construction detail, engineering approval, or installation specification.
