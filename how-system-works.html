How the AmeriDex Dryspace System Works

Integrated PVC decking and Dexerdry seal stop water at the surface and create dry, finished space below.

The Two-Part System

One product. Two engineered components working together.

Cellular PVC Deck Boards

Cellular PVC core with a proprietary ASA cap and a wood-grain finish. A low-maintenance alternative to wood decking.

Dexerdry Integrated Seal

Automotive-grade TPE seal designed to fit between the boards and help direct rain and snowmelt off the deck surface when installed to the specified details.

How It Works in 3 Steps

01

Plan the framing and drainage details.

Use the specified residential joist layout and drainage pitch. Confirm framing condition, flashing, overhang, board direction, and fastening against the current installation instructions.

02

Install AmeriDex boards with the integrated Dexerdry seal.

Interlocking fit creates a continuous water-diverting deck surface.

03

Direct water away from the space below.

The installed board-and-seal assembly directs surface water toward the planned discharge edge. Open-sided areas can still be exposed to wind-driven rain.

New decks and qualifying full re-decks

The seal is installed with the boards, not pushed into a finished deck. A full re-deck may be possible when existing boards are removed and the framing meets the installation requirements. Check the re-deck requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything builders, dealers, and homeowners ask before specifying AmeriDex.

What makes AmeriDex different from other under-deck drainage systems?

AmeriDex combines PVC boards and a Dexerdry seal at the deck surface. Separate above-joist systems place a drainage layer beneath the boards; below-joist systems collect water under the framing. Compare installation location and project requirements.

Can AmeriDex be installed on an existing deck?

AmeriDex is for new decks and qualifying full re-decks. It cannot be added to boards that remain installed. For a re-deck, remove the old boards and have your builder assess framing condition, pitch, flashing, and all installation requirements.

What is the Dexerdry seal and what is it made of?

Dexerdry is the integrated drainage seal that ships between every AmeriDex deck board. It is made from automotive-grade TPE (thermoplastic elastomer), the same material category used in car door and window seals. It is designed to form the water-diverting joint between compatible boards. Correct installation and the specified edge details are essential.

What are AmeriDex deck boards made of?

AmeriDex boards are cellular PVC with a proprietary ASA cap and a wood-grain finish. They are made in the USA. Follow the installation and care instructions for the specified application.

What joist spacing does AmeriDex require?

AmeriDex specifies 16 inches on-center joist spacing for residential applications. Also confirm the required drainage pitch, flashing, board direction, fastening, and perimeter details in the current installation instructions.

What lengths and colors are available?

AmeriDex deck boards are stocked in 12, 16, and 20 foot lengths and in seven PVC colors. Custom lengths are available on request for projects with non-standard runs. You can request free samples to see all seven colors in person.

What is the AmeriDex warranty?

AmeriDex carries a 25-year residential limited warranty and a 10-year limited commercial warranty on the deck board system.

What is the fire rating of AmeriDex deck boards?

AmeriDex lists a Class A flame-spread rating under ASTM E84. Request the applicable report and confirm local requirements with the project authority. This is not, by itself, a statement of wildfire approval or a fire-resistance rating for the complete assembly.
