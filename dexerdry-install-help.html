What is DexerDry?A flexible, coilable thermoplastic weather strip (flange) that fits in the side grooves of grooved deck boards so water sheds off the deck surface and the area below stays much drier.product facts

A flexible, coilable thermoplastic weather strip (flange) that fits in the side grooves of grooved deck boards so water sheds off the deck surface and the area below stays much drier.

It is not rubber.

No, DexerDry is not a channel under the joists; it is an above joist system that sits in the grooves between the deck boards.
